THE IDEA

Look at totals, not individual games.

Let Sd be the number of games played by the end of day d. The cards below are the 22 values S0, S1, …, S21, reduced modulo 21.

01

Twenty-two totals, twenty-one remainders.

There are 22 cumulative totals from day 0 through day 21, but only 21 possible remainders modulo 21. Two totals therefore have the same remainder.

02

Same remainder means a multiple of 21.

If those totals occur at days a and b, then Sb − Sa is a positive multiple of 21.

03

It can only be 21.

Three weeks contain at most 3 × 12 = 36 games. The difference is positive and below 42, so its only possible positive multiple of 21 is 21.

CONCLUSION

Some consecutive days contain exactly 21 games.

The start and end days may vary with the schedule, but the pigeonhole principle guarantees that the interval exists every time.
