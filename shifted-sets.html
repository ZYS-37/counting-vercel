PIGEONHOLE MOMENT

156 entries must collide in 154 slots.

The two sets together have 156 entries, while every entry is an integer from 0 to 153—only 154 possible slots. A value must appear in both sets.

THE COLLISION

One total is exactly 21 ahead of another.

If a common value is Sa in A and Sb + 21 in A + 21, then

Sa = Sb + 21 ⇒ Sa − Sb = 21

So the games played from day b + 1 through day a form a succession of consecutive days with exactly 21 games.
